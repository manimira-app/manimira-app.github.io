Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 July 2026

ManiMira collects no personal data. It has no account system, no analytics, and no networking code of any kind. Your hand photo is examined and your preview is created on your iPhone, and neither is ever transmitted anywhere.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the ManiMira iPhone app. ManiMira is an independent app intended for adults 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and it is not designed for use by them.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store on our systems, sell, or share any personal information. We operate no servers that the app talks to, because the app does not talk to any server.

Your photos

  • ManiMira asks for access to your camera or photo library only when you choose to provide a hand photo, and explains why at that moment.
  • The photo is examined for quality and used to build your preview entirely on your iPhone.
  • The photo is held in memory for your session. It is written to storage only if you explicitly save a result.
  • The photo is never uploaded, never sent to a third party, and never used to train or evaluate any model.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • Previews you explicitly choose to save, in the app's private storage.
  • Your style preferences, stored as style signals — not as photographs.
  • A count of previews used today, so the free daily limit can be applied.

All of this stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

When you share a preview

If you use the share button, iOS shows you the standard share sheet and you choose where the image goes — Photos, Messages, or another app. That copy is then governed by whichever app or service you sent it to, and ManiMira can no longer reach it or delete it. Nothing is shared unless you initiate it.

Third parties

ManiMira contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting services, and no trackers. No third party receives any information about you through this app.

If you download the app from the App Store, Apple handles that transaction under Apple's own privacy policy. We do not receive personal information from Apple about you.

Your controls

  • Delete any saved preview from within the app.
  • Reset your stored preferences from the app's preferences screen.
  • Revoke camera or photo access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove all of its local data.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, export, or erase on our side — a request to delete your data is satisfied entirely by the controls above.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of ManiMira changes how information is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
